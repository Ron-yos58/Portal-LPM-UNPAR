## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, authoritative, and tranquil institutional governance ethos. Tailored for higher education quality assurance directors, auditors, deans, and compliance officers, the aesthetic communicates procedural precision, objective clarity, and administrative reliability without the fatigue of legacy bureaucratic software.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** with **Refined Analytical Minimalism**:
- Pristine, data-dense work surfaces with clear visual hierarchies.
- Calm, refreshing chromatic balance leveraging deep forest teals and restorative mint-gray backdrops.
- Purposeful micro-interactions with crisp card separations, providing clarity for audit trails, compliance matrices, and accreditation scores.
- Restraint over decoration: empty space signifies structure, while vibrant accents spotlight actionable metrics, status changes, and critical compliance variances.

## Colors

The palette balances formal institutional presence with modern product ergonomics, formulated specifically for extended daily analytical work in high-stakes auditing environments.

- **Primary (`#065f46`, `#134e4a`)**: Anchors primary navigation, active menu states, header actions, and key validation buttons. Imparts authority, stability, and institutional permanence.
- **Secondary & Accent (`#0d9488`, `#14b8a6`)**: Drives attention toward progress indicators, key compliance KPIs, interactive tabs, active filters, and interactive data visualizations.
- **Surface & Background (`#f3f8f6`, `#ffffff`)**: Canvas background uses a calming, desaturated mint-gray tint (`#f3f8f6`) which reduces ocular strain during document audits, contrasted sharply by crisp `#ffffff` surface cards.
- **Neutral Hierarchy (`#1e293b`, `#334155`, `#64748b`, `#94a3b8`)**: Charcoal scales guarantee WCAG AAA compliance. `#1e293b` governs primary titles and metrics, `#334155` handles standard body text, and `#64748b` delivers readable metadata, breadcrumbs, and helper annotations.
- **Border Tone (`#e2ece9`, `#cbdad5`)**: Softened, cool-tinted boundaries that reinforce container hierarchy without introducing visual noise.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Success / Compliant: `#059669` (Emerald)
  - Warning / Under Review: `#d97706` (Amber)
  - Critical / Non-Compliant: `#e11d48` (Rose)
  - Information / Standard: `#0284c7` (Sky)

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides the core type system across display, body, and UI labels. Its geometric precision and contemporary apertures ensure effortless reading across dense criteria tables, accreditation matrices, and scoring rubrics.

- **Headlines**: Set with tight, negative tracking to produce cohesive, commanding structural titles that visually anchor dense dashboard screens.
- **Body Text**: Tuned with generous line heights (`1.45rem` - `1.6rem`) to ensure effortless evaluation of institutional decrees, auditor narratives, and regulatory guidelines.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled across all data grids, analytical KPI widgets, and compliance scores to align decimal units precisely.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Form tags, status chips, and table headers adopt medium-to-bold weights with slight positive letter spacing for rapid scanning under high operational velocity.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing an asymmetrical 12-column responsive fluid grid configured for complex administrative workflows.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout with a fixed 280px persistent institutional sidebar, a fluid workspace canvas, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer boundaries. Max content width caps at 1600px for ultrawide panels to preserve scan line discipline.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses into an icon dock (72px) or off-canvas drawer. Gutters scale to 20px, margins to 24px. Multi-column tables activate horizontal inline scroll containers.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with a single-column reflow for cards and metric tiles. Margins reduce to 16px (`1rem`), and gutters scale to 16px (`1rem`). Dense tables transform into stacked summary card views.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Component internals must maintain a strict 2:1 inner-padding-to-gap ratio (e.g., card padding at `space-lg` [24px] paired with row item gaps at `space-md` [16px]).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp, high-clarity structural elevation that balances subtle containment borders with soft, mint-tinted ambient shadows.

- **Base Layer**: Background canvas sits at `#f3f8f6`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Panels)**: Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a 1px solid border in `#e2ece9`, accompanied by an ultra-diffused, cool-tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Hover Cards)**: `#ffffff` surface, 1px border `#cbdad5`, shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Audit Drawers)**: `#ffffff` surface with zero border bleed, paired with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.08)`. Modals deploy over a backdrop blur overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Clickable cards do not jump drastically in depth; instead, their border transitions smoothly to `#14b8a6` with an elevation increase from Level 1 to Level 2 over 150ms ease-out.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a modern, approachable structural geometry characterized by `rounded-2xl` for primary structural elements, offset by tighter radii for internal controls.

- **Primary Cards & Modals**: `1rem` (16px, corresponding to `rounded-2xl`). This softens data-heavy dashboards, providing a sophisticated, human-centered SaaS experience while maintaining analytical rigor.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns)**: `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-md`). Balances ease of touch-targeting with compact horizontal density.
- **Tags, Badges & Pill Counters**: Full circular radii (`9999px`) for status pills, auditor indicators, and criterion scores.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid background in `#065f46`, text in `#ffffff`, subtle hover state shifting to `#134e4a` with 0.5rem corner radii. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#0d9488`.
- **Secondary**: Light background `#f0fdf4`, text in `#065f46`, border 1px solid `#a7f3d0`. Hover shifts to `#dcfce7`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Neutral `#334155` text, transparent background, hovering to `#e2ece9`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Constructed with full pill radii (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical by `space-sm` horizontal padding, paired with `label-sm` bold uppercase typography.
- **Accredited / Fulfilled**: Mint background (`#ecfdf5`), border `#a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`.
- **In Progress / Audit Scheduled**: Cyan/Teal background (`#f0fdfa`), border `#99f6e4`, text `#0f766e`.
- **Needs Improvement / Revision**: Amber background (`#fffbeb`), border `#fde68a`, text `#92400e`.
- **Non-Compliant**: Rose background (`#fff1f2`), border `#fecdd3`, text `#9f1239`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 1px solid border in `#cbdad5`, pure `#ffffff` fill, 0.5rem roundedness, `space-sm` to `space-md` interior padding.
- Focused state: Border transitions to `#0d9488` with a 3px soft outer ring tint (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).
- Error state: Border `#e11d48`, focus ring tinted rose. Accompanying helper text rendered in `body-sm` rose.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), 1.5px border `#94a3b8`.
- Checked state: `#065f46` solid fill with white checkmark glyph. Checkbox corners use `4px` smooth radius.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- Surface background `#ffffff`, 1rem border radius (`rounded-2xl`), 1px solid `#e2ece9` perimeter, and Level 1 elevation shadow.
- Standard card header includes an icon badge in `#f0fdfa` with a `#0d9488` SVG icon, an explicit headline in `headline-sm`, and an optional right-aligned status chip or action menu.

### Data Tables (Audit & Indicator Matrices)
- Alternating subtle row zebra striping (hover row background `#f8fafc`).
- Sticky table header with `#f8fafc` background, uppercase `label-sm` text in `#64748b`, separated by a crisp bottom border `#e2ece9`.
- Integrated progress bars inside indicator cells utilizing `#0d9488` bars on `#e2ece9` tracks.
